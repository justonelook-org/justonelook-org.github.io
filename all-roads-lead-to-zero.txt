ALL ROADS LEAD TO ZERO

Now that Zero is in place, the next step is to direct traffic to Zero. This initiative has come to be called “All Roads Lead to Zero.”

Zero now has two clean public URLs:

Look At Yourself — Step One:
https://justonelook.org/try-it/

Self-Directed Attention Exercise — Step Two:
https://justonelook.org/self-directed-attention/

For the currently published social media outreach, we have two clean source links:

X:
https://justonelook.org/try-it/x/

Bluesky:
https://justonelook.org/try-it/bluesky/

These links take the visitor directly to Zero’s Look At Yourself guide.

Visits appear on the private dashboard as separate anonymous X and Bluesky totals. Additional social sources or campaign routes should be implemented only when their public links are ready to use.

The tracking is aggregate and anonymous. It does not identify individual visitors or save their conversations. Its purpose is only to give us a simple picture of where traffic to Zero is coming from so that we can make better decisions about future social media outreach.
