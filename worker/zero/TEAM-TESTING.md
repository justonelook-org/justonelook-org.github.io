# JOL team access to Zero

The JOL team should test Zero on the separate test website:

**Looking Zero:** <https://website-test-zero.pages.dev/try-it/>

**Self-Directed Attention Zero:** <https://website-test-zero.pages.dev/self-directed-attention/>

Please use these links for team review, demonstrations, repeated tests, and experiments. Do not use the public Just One Look website for routine testing.

The test website runs the same Zero guidance, but its sessions are excluded from the production Looking Zero outcome dashboard. This keeps team activity from being mixed with visitor data.

Do not share the test links publicly. If something behaves unexpectedly, note the date, approximate time, which Zero you used, and what happened. Do not copy private visitor information into a report.
