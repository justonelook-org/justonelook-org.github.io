Research paper

The Radical Act of Inward Looking

Paul Freedman, M.S.W., R.S.W.; Jonathan Goldberg, M.S.W., R.S.W.; and Jaak Reichmann, M.D., FRCP(C)

This paper examines the Act of Inward Looking as a behavioral intervention for psychological suffering. It considers the method through the lens of in vivo exposure therapy and contrasts it with mindfulness-based approaches.

Originally published in Undivided: The Online Journal of Nonduality and Psychology, Vol. 1, Issue 4.

Preliminary research note · Psychology

An Unpublished Study of the Act of Inward Looking

Niklas Lindström · Master’s thesis in progress

Preliminary pilot data from an unfinished randomized study suggest that a brief written instruction in the Act of Inward Looking may be worth further investigation. The study tested the instruction itself, without JOL branding, personal guidance, or author involvement. The small sample and inconclusive results do not establish an effect, but the numerical pattern supports completing the thesis and conducting further research.

Unpublished and unfinished study. The study design and data collection were approved, but the thesis and complete statistical analysis have not yet been completed.
